How this works

Creator activity is shown when at least 3 creators have confirmed activity and the sample reaches emerging (5 or more confirmed events). Below that, not enough data.

Reply rate is shown when at least 15 confirmed pitches from at least 5 creators fall in the last 365 days. A follow-up is not another pitch. Below that, not enough data.

Typical reply time is shown when at least 10 confirmed replies from at least 5 creators fall in the last 365 days. The value is a time band, not an hour count. Below that, not enough data.

Confidence and freshness sit beside a metric. They are not a score. Insufficient data is never 0% and never Low.
