ILLUSTRATIVE CONTENT COLLABORATION AGREEMENT

This illustrative agreement is between the hotel and creator for an editorial collaboration taking place November 12–15, 2026.

DELIVERABLES. The creator will provide two short-form vertical videos, B-roll footage and ten edited photographs.

COMPENSATION. The hotel will provide a three-night accommodation stay. No cash compensation is specified.

USAGE RIGHTS. The hotel may publish approved content on its own organic social media channels for six months. Paid advertising use requires separate written approval.

REVISIONS. One round of reasonable edits to each video is included.

TERMINATION. Either party may cancel with fourteen days written notice, subject to mutually agreed treatment of work completed.
